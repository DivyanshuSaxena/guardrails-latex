\begin{itemize}
    \item Why OSes will see more and more learned policies - learned policies do better than stock OS policies.
    \item Reluctance of OS practitioners in using learned models - overheads, decision quality, etc. For decision quality, primarily because of the black box nature and the lack of guarantees. 
    \item Need for guardrails to make learned policies more usable in OSes.
    \item We propose the abstraction of guardrails, and discuss their applicability to three different OS components.
    \item Easier to come up with properties, than coming up with actual algo: that's why human created heuristics will fail, but human created specs shall prevail. 
\end{itemize}
