\section{Background}\label{sec:background}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Learned policies can be significantly better than stock OS {\it on average}. Demonstrate with examples.
    \item However, sometimes the models may misbehave - in particular, they do not come with any guarantees. Standardized API; taxonomy.
    \item Examples of the type of guarantees that are needed in OSes.
    \item Coming up with these guarantees is easier than the full algorithm.
\end{itemize}
