\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

%\section{Discussion}

Performance introspection is the central idea of this paper; guardrails realize it for properties that admit run-time checks over OS-visible observations and lightweight online responses. This excludes objectives that require long horizons, external application-level metrics, or interventions too expensive to perform during execution. Supporting such objectives would require richer cross-layer observability, longer-horizon state and reasoning, and mechanisms for deferred or offline adaptation.

Performance introspection is broader than the OS. We focus on the OS because it is a rich and difficult setting: it has many performance-critical policies, already suffers from brittle specialization, and lacks visibility into the end-to-end objectives. These constraints make the OS a compelling instantiation, while broader extensions beyond the OS remain promising future work.
